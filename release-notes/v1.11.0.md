## Summary
When E-Control itself is down, Home Assistant now says so in a Repairs notice instead of leaving you to suspect your setup. The card also handles small and resized dashboard cells better: it scrolls instead of cutting stations off, it fits half-width columns, and a removed card no longer keeps polling price history.

## Breaking changes
_None._ Entity IDs, unique IDs, attributes and card options are unchanged.

## Added
- **E-Control outage notice.** Once E-Control has failed on its side (server errors, an unusable answer or its maintenance page) at least twice over 10 minutes, a Repairs issue under **Settings → System → Repairs** says the service is down. It clears with the first real answer. On the card, an unavailable sensor now shows "Can't reach E-Control" instead of "No data available".

## Changed
- In a sections view, a card dragged to fewer rows than it needs now scrolls between its fuel tabs and its footer. Before, the stations below the cut were simply hidden.
- On a half-width card (below 280 px) each station takes two lines: name and address on top, price and buttons underneath. Wider cards keep one line per station.
- Turning off **Fit height to content** now starts the card at a height that matches its content instead of a fixed 6 units, and masonry dashboards balance their columns with it.

## Fixed
- A long station name no longer hides the **Closed** / **Closing soon** label; the name ends in "…" instead.
- A card removed from a dashboard stopped fetching price history in the background. Before, it kept fetching 28 days of history for every sensor every 30 minutes until the browser tab was reloaded.
- The add-card picker shows the card's preview at its own height instead of stretching it to the tallest preview in the row.
- The card editor's live preview no longer shows the version-update banner, whose Reload button would have thrown away the edit in progress.
- The card switches language right away when you change your profile language, instead of waiting for the next sensor update.
- Removing an entry also removes its "Location tracker unavailable" Repairs issue.
- Keyboard focus rings on the fuel tabs and station rows are no longer cut off at the card's edges.

## Compatibility
- Home Assistant: ≥ 2025.1
- HACS: ≥ 2.0.0

- Card lint gained the vitest test-hygiene rules; new card suites cover the manual-refresh cooldown, history polling, the grid-cell rules and the card-size estimate.
- Dependency updates: ruff 0.17.3, mypy, pre-commit hooks, vite-plus 1.4.0, happy-dom 21 and the GitHub Actions group (#105, #106, #107).
